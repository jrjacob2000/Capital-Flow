Privacy Policy for CapitalFlow

Effective Date: October 21, 2026 • Last Updated: October 21, 2026

CapitalFlow ("we", "our", or "the Application") is a personal income, expense, and savings ledger web application hosted at https://jrjacob2000.github.io/Capital-Flow/. This Privacy Policy explains what information CapitalFlow collects, how we use and store that information, and how we comply with Google API Services User Data Policy.

1. Information We Collect and Access

When you use CapitalFlow, we only access the minimum information required to provide the application's features:

  • Basic Google Profile Information: When you sign in with Google Authentication, we receive your basic profile information (display name, email address, and profile avatar URL) solely to display your active signed-in session inside the application interface.
  • App-Created Google Drive Files (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): CapitalFlow requests the non-sensitive drive.file scope, which grants access exclusively to files and folders created or opened by CapitalFlow in your own Google Drive account (specifically the /CapitalFlow/ folder containing your JSON budget, template, and savings ledger files). CapitalFlow cannot view, list, read, modify, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • Local Browser Storage: Your budget entries, templates, and savings ledger entries are stored locally in your browser's localStorage so you can work offline and sync on demand.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information and financial ledger data are used strictly for the following user-facing purposes:

  • Saving, reading, and backing up your personal budget JSON files (e.g., capitalflow_budget_YYYY.json), budget templates (capitalflow_budget_template.json), and savings ledger (capitalflow_savings_ledger.json) directly inside your personal Google Drive account.
  • Displaying your name, email address, and avatar in the application header so you know which Google account is currently connected.
  • Comparing timestamps between your local browser storage and your Google Drive backup files to prevent accidental data overwrites.

3. Data Storage, Sharing, and Third-Party Disclosure

  • Zero External Server Storage: CapitalFlow is a client-side web application. Your financial data flows directly between your web browser and your personal Google Drive account via official Google APIs. We do not operate a backend database that stores your financial records.
  • No Selling or Sharing of Data: We never sell, rent, trade, or transfer your Google user data or financial data to any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • No AI Training: Your Google user data and Google Drive files are never used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure)

CapitalFlow's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Retention, Revocation, and Deletion

  • Full User Control: Because your backup files reside in your own Google Drive under the /CapitalFlow/ folder, you retain 100% ownership and control over your data at all times. You may view, export, or permanently delete these files directly from your Google Drive at any time.
  • Clearing Local Data: You can clear your local browser data at any time using the "Reset Data" option inside the application or by clearing your browser's site storage.
  • Revoking Google Access: You may sign out within the app at any time or revoke CapitalFlow's access to your Google account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Security

All communication between your browser, Firebase Authentication, and Google Drive APIs is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. OAuth 2.0 access tokens are held only in your browser session for direct requests to Google's APIs.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or CapitalFlow's data practices, please contact the developer at: jrjacob2000@gmail.com.
